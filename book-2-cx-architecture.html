Insight 01
Omnichannel Is a Routing Problem, Not a Channel Problem
Most omnichannel failures trace back to treating each channel as a separate implementation project. The book establishes a unified routing architecture as the foundation — one that makes adding channels additive rather than multiplying complexity.
Insight 02
Product Architecture Determines Long-Term TCO
The technical decisions made in the first ninety days of a Genesys Cloud CX deployment determine the total cost of ownership across the next five years. This book maps those decisions with their downstream consequences — giving architects the leverage to get them right the first time.

Chapter 1 — Free Preview The Architect's Contract

An architect's contract is not with the vendor. It is not with the technology. It is with the organization's future operational reality — the call center that will process ten thousand interactions a day on whatever you design today, staffed by people who had no part in the design conversation.

Genesys Cloud CX is a powerful platform. But power without architecture produces complexity, and complexity in a contact center produces one outcome with predictable reliability: abandoned calls, frustrated agents, and a project postmortem that blames the technology for decisions the architecture made.

The work of this book is to give you — the architect, the implementation lead, the technical product manager — the precise framework for translating business intent into architectural decisions, and architectural decisions into implementation patterns that the organization can actually sustain. That translation is the architect's craft. This book treats it as one.
