Insight 01
The Breadcrumb Is Already There
Customers do not withhold their behavior — they signal it in every click, pause, abandon, and return. The gap is not data volume; it is instrumentation discipline. This book begins with what organizations already have and shows how to make it speak.
Insight 02
Journey Analytics Is Not Web Analytics
Session data shows what a customer did in a single visit. Journey analytics shows what a customer experienced across twelve weeks, four channels, and three handoffs. The two disciplines require fundamentally different instrumentation models — and most organizations are running the wrong one.
Insight 03
Actionable Insight Has an Expiry Date
The customer who was confused at the self-service portal at 2pm cannot be helped by an insight report published next Tuesday. This book covers the architectural pattern that makes journey data actionable in the moment that matters — not after the customer has already left.

Chapter 1 — Free Preview The Anatomy of a Breadcrumb

A digital breadcrumb is not a data point. It is an event with context: a customer action, attached to an identity, a timestamp, a channel, a session state, and a prior history that determines what the action means. Strip the context and you have a log entry. Keep the context and you have the beginning of a conversation the organization can have with its own data.

Most organizations strip the context. They collect events — page views, clicks, call arrivals, chat initiations — in systems optimized for storage, not interpretation. The data warehouse fills up with timestamps. The customer journey remains invisible.

The transformation this book describes begins with a choice made at instrumentation time: to capture not just what happened, but who it happened to, where they had been before, and what they were trying to do. That choice, made consistently across channels and touchpoints, is the precondition for everything that follows — the analytics, the models, the interventions that turn passive data into active CX.
