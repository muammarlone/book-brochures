Chapter 1 — Free Preview The Architecture of Intent

Every enterprise CX transformation begins with the same conversation: which platform should we choose? It is the wrong question. The right question — the one that determines whether the investment succeeds or quietly fails over eighteen months — is: what architecture of intent are we building toward?

Intent, in this context, is not a vision statement or a strategic objective. It is the set of precise, verifiable design decisions that determine how technology, people, and process interact at the moment a customer makes contact. Platforms are interchangeable in a way that intent is not. Genesys, Salesforce, and Amazon Connect all route calls. Only one of them routes calls in accordance with your governance structure, your data model, and the customer journey you have actually committed to delivering.

The framework in this book begins here — before vendor selection, before technical architecture, before the first training session. It begins with the discipline of defining what you are building toward before you decide what you are building with.
